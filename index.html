How it works

  1. Describe the symptom as you see it. You don't need the exact warning code.
  2. If several causes are possible, MEC-AI asks up to 3 questions. Tap a quick reply or type your own.
  3. MEC-AI only searches tips with status Approved. Pending submissions are left out.
  4. Every answer shows who fixed the problem, the photo, video or voice note, when it was approved and the aircraft system.
  5. Always confirm the procedure in the official aircraft manual before working on it.
